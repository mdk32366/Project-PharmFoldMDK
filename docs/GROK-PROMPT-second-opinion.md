**The project.** A master's ML project, "PharmFoldMDK." It folds the extracellular domains (ECDs) of cancer cell-surface antigens with ESMFold (single-sequence, no MSA), computes six geometric features per ECD (ECD length, radius of gyration, mean pLDDT, membrane-proximal pLDDT, normalized SASA, largest-patch fraction), and fits an L2 logistic regression to rank targets by "ADC-suitability." The label is "has this antigen been attempted as an ADC target" (Group B, 12 curated positives). The comparator is an expression-and-selectivity ranking from Kathad et al. 2024 (PLOS ONE, e0308604), whose 82-target cohort is reused. Scoring uses leave-one-out percentiles.

**The pre-registered result (F-004).** On 12 labelled positives: LOO percentile median 0.607, 8/12 above 0.5. Head-to-head vs. the expression comparator on 8 targets: structural mean 0.573 / median 0.625 vs. comparator 0.563 / 0.750 — deemed "not distinguishable," direction reverses between mean and median. Spearman(structural, comparator) = -0.048. Stated finding: "the structural axis ranks attempted-ADC targets modestly above chance, is not distinguishable from the comparator, and is not a proxy for it — at n=12 it measures something different but cannot be shown to add anything." A named open confound: two features are pLDDT-derived, and ESMFold confidence may track training-data representation, which tracks research attention, which tracks having-been-attempted-as-an-ADC.
