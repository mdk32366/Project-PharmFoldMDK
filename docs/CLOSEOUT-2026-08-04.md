## The three findings that matter most

**1. The census denominator is 2,807, not 2,886.** Keyed by accession — as every join in this
project is — the surface class collapses 79 rows into four HLA loci UniProt has since merged
(HLA-B 35, HLA-A 21, HLA-C 14, HLA-DRB1 13). ⚠ **The Planner had labelled 2,886 VERIFIED**, having
counted the file. The count was right; **it answered the wrong question.** A provenance label says
where a number came from and nothing about whether it is the quantity needed. **Every census count
now states its key.**

**2. `~5,102` was wrong in a worse way than being unverified.** It was Planner arithmetic
(2,886 + 2,216) resting on an unstated assumption — *the classes partition the table* — and produced
something internally consistent and plausible. **A number that is wrong and consistent is far harder
to catch than one that is wrong and odd.** Withdrawn, not corrected: no corrected version exists.

**3. Commensurability is now a named open question.** `fold_provenance` shows the 80 folded rows
span **three recipes** — `('int8',64)×42`, `('fp16',None)×34`, `('fp16',64)×3`, one unrecorded.
⚠ **F-012 measured chunk-size variation; the cohort's variable is chunked-versus-not.** Adjacent
questions; the cohort's has never been tested. **No claim in either direction** — *"those 34 folds
are fine"* is exactly as unsupported as the opposite. **D-075 Decision 6 gains a fourth item and
P-001's limitations section must carry it at F-005 strength.** Found by us, before review.

proves the assertion bites.* **No F-004 or F-005 guard was proven by a fake red** and the affected
guard was re-proven — but the claim must be made in its corrected form: *the revert must be a
realistic mistake and must fail at the assertion.*
