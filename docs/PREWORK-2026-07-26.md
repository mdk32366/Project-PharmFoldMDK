## §1 — The ruling that shapes the whole day: what "the last UI elements" may be

The four items on the horizon from last session are **Story tab, Architecture tab, ADC mechanism
animation, per-target cancer associations.** Taken at face value, all four are prose and pictures.
None of them touches the network, and a day spent entirely on them advances the *narrative* while
the Prime Directive question — *"where is the deep learning, and does our system use it in a
defensible way?"* — gets no new evidence.

I do not think that means "don't build them." I think it means **the justification has to be the
real one, and the build discipline has to match it.** The real one is this:

> The deep learning in this project is ESMFold, and it has already done its work — 79 folds, each
> with the network's own per-residue confidence. What the last UI elements do is make that work
> **locatable and bounded**: a reader must be able to find the neural network in thirty seconds,
> and must be told precisely where it stops. That is the same discipline as D-016 and D-024,
> applied to the deliverable itself rather than to a number inside it.

That justification is defensible in a decision entry. It also imposes two hard constraints on
today's work, which I am proposing as binding:

- **Constraint A — no narrative surface may carry a hardcoded cohort statistic.** D-050 was decided
  *nine days* before delivery precisely because prose that quotes a fold count rots. A Story tab
  that says "we folded 79 targets" as a literal re-arms the exact trap D-050 disarmed. Every count,
  range, and fraction on a new surface is **derived from `/api/analyses` or `/api/coverage`**, or it
  is stated qualitatively with no number at all.
- **Constraint B — the Architecture tab must be checkable, or it is decoration.** A hand-drawn
  system picture is a claim with no provenance; it will be wrong within one PR of any route change
  and nobody will notice. See §3 for the mechanism I propose: a committed system model that a
  **Python test asserts against the live FastAPI route table**, so adding or removing a route
  reddens the gate until the diagram is updated. That converts the diagram from a picture into an
  artefact under the same discipline as everything else here.

**Ruling needed from you:** accept the framing and both constraints, or amend them. Everything in
§3 is written assuming you accept.

Architecture. Honest, simple, and the entry records why the audience changed (a professor meeting

**(B) Five destinations, by absorbing rather than adding — recommended.**

|---|---|---|

| Targets | `/targets` | moved from `/` |

| About ADCs | `/about` | **absorbs the mechanism schematic** — the page already describes this mechanism in prose |

(B) is better on three counts: it respects the plan's constraint instead of overriding it; it puts

### D-051 — The narrative surfaces: the Story, and an Architecture diagram that is checkable

**Two parts, one decision** (the D-048 precedent for a multi-part UI entry).

**Part 1 — the Story surface.** A cold-open narrative at `/`: the research question, what ESMFold
is and that *we ran it*, what came out (derived counts — Constraint A), what did not fold and why,
and what is deliberately not built yet (the scorer and the ranking, D-028's non-goals as
commitments). It is the thirty-second answer to "where is the deep learning."

*Tests (user tests, vitest, tests-first):* a fixture `/api/analyses` + `/api/coverage` payload with
**distinctive** values that cannot coincide with today's live cohort (the D-050 fixture discipline —
e.g. 4 folded, range 40.00–88.00) → the rendered counts match the fixture; and a negative assertion
that the literals `79`, `82`, `84.23`, `30.68` do **not** appear as hardcoded text. That second test
is the one that keeps Constraint A enforced after we stop looking.

**Part 2 — the Architecture diagram, pinned to the running system.** Hand-rolled SVG (D-037
precedent: hand-rolled over a chart library in the weaker-guarantee dependency world), rendered
from a **committed system model** rather than drawn:

- `ui/src/system-model.json` — the nodes (Fly serving tier, Postgres, Volume, local GPU worker,
  rental tier), the edges, and two explicit route lists: the public `/api/*` reads and the
  bearer-guarded `/jobs/*` worker routes.
- `ArchitectureDiagram.jsx` imports it and renders it. No route names typed into JSX.
- **`tests/test_architecture_contract.py` (functional test, Python):** loads the JSON, builds the
  FastAPI app the way `tests/test_ui_serving.py` already does, walks `app.routes`, and asserts the
  two sets are **equal in both directions** — every real route appears in the model, every modelled
  route is real. Precedent for a test reading a non-Python artefact as data:
  `tests/test_image_contents.py` reads the `Dockerfile` as text.

*Red-then-green proof for Part 2:* before wiring the model, add a deliberately wrong entry (a
`/api/ranking` route that does not exist) → the contract test must go **red**; remove it → green.
A test that never went red is not confirmed to bite.

*Deep-learning justification:* the diagram's load-bearing job is to show **where inference runs and
where it does not** — GPU tier outside Fly, serving tier GPU-free (D-004, DEP-001). That topology is
the single most-asked question about this system and the most easily mis-drawn. Pinning it to the
route table is what keeps the answer true.

### D-052 — The ADC mechanism schematic: an illustration, structurally prevented from reading as our output

An SVG antibody–linker–payload schematic inside `AdcContext` (`/about`), beside the prose that
already names those three parts.

**The risk this entry exists to close:** a reader who has just seen a 3Dmol structure coloured by
pLDDT, and then sees a drawn antibody, may take the drawing for something the system produced. That
is a false claim about the neural network's output — the precise class of error the whole provenance
layer exists to prevent.

*Ruling:* the schematic carries a visible label — *"Schematic illustration. Not a structure produced
by this system; see any target for a real ESMFold output."* — and it is **structurally incapable**
of being a model output: no API import, no props from an analysis.

*Tests (vitest):* the label renders; the component makes **no** call into `api.js` (assert with a
mocked module that no export was invoked — the mechanism, not just the words); a link to a real
folded target is present.

*Deep-learning justification:* negative and protective. It keeps the boundary of the DL claim sharp
at the one place a reader is most likely to blur it.

### D-053 — Per-target cancer associations: curated, cited, and honest about absence

**Scope-gated — see §4. Only the schema/loader/route/UI is Builder work; the roster is yours.**

Follows `adc_reference_mapping.csv` exactly: `data/cancer_associations.csv`, one row per target,
columns `symbol, association, source_citation`; **no incidence statistics, no live retrieval**
(ruled 07-25 prework §4); the loader **rejects an uncited row** the way `load_mapping` does; the
symbol→accession join runs against `data/cohort_82_mapping.csv` so a miss is *flagged, never
silent*. Served by a new public read route (`GET /api/associations`), consistent with D-038's
precedent that a new supplier is its own route, tests-first.

*Renders honestly:* a target with no curated row shows **"no association recorded"**, never a blank
cell and never an inferred one. The panel states its own denominator — *n of 82 curated* — derived,
not typed.

*Migration trigger, recorded now:* this moves to a database table **only** if it becomes a scorer
feature or a query filter. Until then it is a file.

*Deep-learning justification:* the network says how confidently it folded a chain; it says nothing
about why the chain matters. The association is the "why" a reader needs to weigh a pLDDT of 30.68
against one of 84.23 — and stating it *only where a citation exists* is the same discipline as the
17-of-82 evidence scores, applied to domain content.

encounters it, which is what makes them user tests rather than unit tests. A new surface with a
