## ⚠ The four that would have produced a plausible wrong artifact

**1. `geom_proxy` would have fitted a fabricated result.** Feature 7 was NULL on all 80 rows;
`fit_scorer.py:111`'s `or 0.0` coerced it, `scorer.py:152` standardised zero variance to 0.0, and the
run would have landed at Decision 4's **ambiguous row — the one the log names as expected at n=12 —
for the wrong reason.** Same `run_kind`, plausible triple, 56 warnings scrolled off above it, nothing
red. **Found by Code, post-migration.**

**2. The remedy for (1) was itself unsafe.** The Planner prescribed
`extract_features.py --all --load`, which **INSERTs** — 80 rows would have become 160, features 1–6
rewritten, and the new rows bound to `plddt_only` by a default nobody chose. **Found by the Planner,
verifying its own prescription against the tree.**

**3. Task 2's output schema broke Task 3's consumer, silently.** No `accession` column, no bucket
equal to `resolved` — so every span fetch would have been skipped and **the band split would have
read `no_topology` for the entire census.** ⚠ A resolvable target recorded as having no topology is
**fabrication, not smoothing** — a new shape for this project. **Found by Code, in committed code.**

**4. Task C's stop condition would have halted on the correct outcome.** It conflated the fill's
population (80) with the guard's (56). **Found by Code, before execution.**

## ⚠ The two methodological results

**A-017 (the fixture must discriminate).** Five instances, two agents, three tasks, one day. **(a)**
the fixture must reach the code; **(b)** each property needs its own test, since a compound test
proves only its first failing assertion; **(c)** the fixture must contain a case where correct and
incorrect differ. **Red-then-green rested on two unstated assumptions and both were found by someone
reverting their own work and looking at where the red actually fired.**

**F-022.** *Two independent readings* corroborated 0007 twice, the census denominators, and the
determinism result. ⚠ **But independence of readers is not independence of reasoning.** Both parties
read the same dry-run line, both took an extraction-time report for a persisted field, and agreed.
**What caught it was writing the expectation down where it could be falsified — not the second
reader.**
