### 9.1 ⚠⚠ RULED: the 37 and the collapse wait for the fold to finish

Asked during the session whether the re-fold could run alongside slice 3. **No.** Two reasons, the
second decisive.

1. ⚠ **It would contaminate the run's only deliverable.** `run_worker` claims the next job of its
   **tier**, not *one of mine* — that is the stranger guard's premise. The 37 are spans 29–30,
   band 1–30; slice 3 is band 31–100. If they become claimable mid-run the live worker takes them,
   and the wall times and transport term mix two bands. **The transport term for 31–100 is what the
   five-hour run exists to produce.**
2. ⚠⚠ **The collapse must not run during the fold at all.** It builds a unique index against a table
   the worker updates on every completion. Index builds take locks. Real risk of disrupting a run
   40% through.

**Both after the fold lands. Same tunnel, same sitting. Roughly ten minutes of folding plus the
collapse — a short sitting, but not this one.**
