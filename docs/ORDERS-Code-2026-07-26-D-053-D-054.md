### D-054 — The evidence baseline is deferred, on purpose, with a trigger

- **Date:** 2026-07-26
- **Status:** Accepted — **a deferral, not an omission.**
- **Relates:** D-040 (the evidence scores and their 17-of-82 curation gap), D-024 (the honest
  denominator), D-028 (non-goals as commitments), UI Plan v2 §8 (the ranking centrepiece).

**Context.** `data/evidence_scores.csv` holds 17 curated, cited rows — 9 scoring 4, 8 scoring 5 —
and is reachable from no route and no component. It is the published comparator half of the research
question, and it is invisible in the deployed product.

**Decision.** It stays invisible **this session**, and this entry exists so that fact is a recorded
choice rather than an oversight discovered later.

- **It is not blocked by the scorer.** A supplier and one view would ship it. It is blocked by
  judgement: it is one half of the centrepiece, and this project has declined to build the
  centrepiece in halves every session since UI Plan v2 §8. Doing it days before delivery would break
  that discipline at the exact moment the discipline is worth most.
- **The misreading risk is live.** Seventeen rows scoring only 4 and 5 read as *"these are the good
  ones."* The other 65 are **unpublished, not low.** Rendering the 17 without that denominator
  hard-wired into the surface would teach a reader something false — and the reader in question is a
  grader.

**Trigger — the next session, or the scorer fit, whichever comes first.** When built, it carries
D-024's denominator treatment: 17 of 82, the 65 rendered null-with-reason, and it is labelled the
**published comparator**, never "our ranking."

- **Deep-learning justification:** none, and that is the point. This surface would show a
  *published expression-derived* baseline. Its whole value is as the thing the structure-derived
  ranking is measured *against* — which means it is worth little until there is a ranking to
  compare, and actively misleading if shown alone.
- **Consequences:** none in code. A named debt with a trigger, so it cannot quietly vanish.

### D-053 — Per-target cancer associations, derived from the cohort's own source, including where the derivation disagrees

- **Date:** 2026-07-26
- **Status:** Proposed → Accepted on merge.
- **Relates:** D-040 (curated-and-cited discipline; *a different number is a finding, not a
  discrepancy to reconcile away*), D-016 (a claim names how it is known), D-050 (derived, not
  hardcoded), D-038 (a new supplier is its own route), D-024 (the honest denominator), D-052 (the
  boundary between what we produced and what we illustrate).
- **Amends:** the 2026-07-25 pre-work §4, which scoped this as a hand-curated literature roster
  requiring an owner curation pass. **Superseded:** a better source was found — the cohort's own
  paper — making the roster derivable rather than assembled. Recording the reversal explicitly.

**Context.** ESMFold says how confidently it folded a chain. It says nothing about why the chain
matters. Without that, a reader cannot weigh a pLDDT of 30.68 against one of 84.23 — both are just
numbers about proteins they have no reason to care about.

**Decision 1 — derive from the source paper, do not assemble from literature.** The Kathad
supplementary S3 File publishes a complete 82 × 20 grid of per-target, per-tumour-type **quasi
H-scores** (0–300; %low×1 + %med×2 + %high×3, from HPA IHC data). Applying **the paper's own stated
150 cutoff** yields **337 target–tumour pairs covering all 82 targets**. This is strictly better
provenance than a hand-assembled literature roster: one source, one citation, uniform coverage, no
selection by us, and reproducible from a file anyone can download.

**Decision 2 — the claim is an EXPRESSION claim, and the surface says so.** A row means *"highly
expressed in this tumour type, by the paper's measure."* It is **not** causal, **not** a claim the
target drives the disease, and **not** a clinical indication. This bound is rendered, not assumed.

**Decision 3 — record the disagreement; do not reconcile it away.** The disqualifying check was
run before the artefact was trusted:

| Paper states | This derivation | |
|---|---|---|
| OSMR overexpressed in **10** indications | **10** | reproduced exactly |
| **290** target–indication combinations | **337** | not reproduced |
| **16** targets above cutoff in >7 tumour types | **17** | not reproduced |

A strict `>150` cutoff gives 285 pairs — nearer the headline — but breaks OSMR (7, not 10), so it is
the *wrong* reading despite the closer number. Dropping any single tumour type yields 296–334, never
290. S7 File has 213 rows, also not 290. **Conclusion: 290 is a different quantity, produced by a
filtering step the published files do not expose.** Therefore this is *our* derivation from the
paper's published scores — agreeing with one named claim, disagreeing with two — and it is labelled
that way wherever it appears. It is never rendered as "the paper's 290 combinations."

**Decision 4 — every association renders, ranked.** No top-N truncation (owner ruling). BTN3A3 has
16. Legibility is bought by **descending quasi H-score with the score shown**, which makes the list
ordered evidence rather than an unordered dump — so the ordering is a **tested contract**, not
styling.

**Decision 5 — a citation may be a literal; a statistic may not.** This sharpens D-050. The paper's
`290` and `16` are facts about a published document: static, and correct as literals. Our `337`,
`82`, and every per-target count are statistics over a file that can change: they derive from
`/api/associations`. **The test of which kind a number is: would it change if our data changed?**

- **Deep-learning justification:** the network's output is a confidence surface over a structure;
  its significance to a reader is entirely external to it. This supplies that significance from a
  cited source while stating precisely what the citation does and does not support — keeping the
  domain claim and the model claim separable, which is the same boundary D-052 defends in pictures.
- **Consequences:** `data/cancer_associations.csv` (337 rows + provenance header);
  `core/cancer_associations.py`; `GET /api/associations`; a `CancerAssociations` component in
  `TargetView`; tests at both tiers. `data/` is already in the image (`Dockerfile:38`) — no
  packaging change, no new dependency.
- **Migration trigger:** moves to a DB table **only** if it becomes a scorer feature or a query
  filter. Until then it is a file.
- **Correction folded in:** `data/cohort_82.txt` cites its source as *"S3, sheet
  `Target_expression_in_normal`."* S3's only sheet is `Target_expression_in_tumor`; the normal-tissue
  grid is **S2**. D-040 was right. The 82 symbols are identical either way, so **the cohort of record
  is unaffected** — a provenance-label error, not a data error. Fix the label, note it as a
  correction.

## 1. What to build

**Supplier — `core/cancer_associations.py`, pure, no DB, no network.** Mirror
`core/adc_reference.py`'s shape and discipline:

- `load_associations(path)` → parses, **rejects any row with an empty `source_citation`** (the
  D-040 rule: an uncited row is not data), rejects a non-numeric or out-of-range `qh_score`.
- Groups by symbol, each group **sorted by `qh_score` descending**. Sorting belongs here, not in
  JSX — it is a property of the data contract, and it is what makes "all of them" legible.
- Joins symbols against `data/cohort_82_mapping.csv`; symbols with no cohort match are returned in
  an `unmatched_symbols` list. **Flagged, never dropped.** (Verified zero today — the test must
  still prove the mechanism with a deliberately unmatched fixture symbol.)

**Route — `GET /api/associations`, public** (no bearer; `/api` prefix, per the auth contract
`test_ui_serving.py` already pins). Returns the whole map — 337 rows is ~30 KB, and one route for
the whole picture matches D-038. Shape:

```
source, method, cutoff, pair_count, targets_covered, cohort_size,
unmatched_symbols[], associations{ SYMBOL: [ {cancer, qh_score}, ... ] }
```

`pair_count` / `targets_covered` / `cohort_size` are **computed from what was actually loaded**,
never constants.

**UI — `CancerAssociations.jsx`, rendered inside `TargetView`.** Ranked list, score shown.

## 2. ⚠ Four things that will bite if missed

**(a) Do not reuse the pLDDT band palette.** `plddt.js` colours mean *model confidence*. A
quasi H-score is a completely different quantity from a different source. Rendering it in the same
colours teaches a reader they are the same kind of number — the D-052 error class, arriving through
CSS instead of a drawing. Use a visually distinct treatment and do not import from `plddt.js`.

**(b) The panel must state the claim boundary in the rendered output**, not only in the entry:
expression, by the paper's measure — not causation, not indication. A test asserts the boundary text
is present.

**(c) `pair_count` is a statistic; `290` is a citation.** Decision 5. The derivation-gap note may
name the paper's 290 and 16 as literals. It must **not** hardcode 337, 82, or any per-target count —
those come from the payload. A negative test enforces it.

**(d) The absent case must render even though it cannot happen today.** All 82 targets are covered,
so the "no association recorded" branch is currently unreachable in production — which is exactly
when it rots. Test it with a fixture target missing from the map. It must render the absence, never
a blank panel and never a crash.

## 3. Test surface — write these first

**Functional (pytest), `tests/test_cancer_associations.py`:**
1. a row with an empty `source_citation` is **rejected** (red-then-green: assert it loads *before*
   the guard exists, then that it doesn't);
2. a non-numeric / out-of-range `qh_score` is rejected;
3. groups come back sorted by `qh_score` **descending** — fixture with deliberately out-of-order
   input rows and distinctive scores;
4. a fixture symbol absent from `cohort_82_mapping.csv` lands in `unmatched_symbols` and is not
   silently dropped;
5. `pair_count` equals rows actually loaded — 5-row fixture → 5, not a constant;
6. **the real file loads clean**: 337 pairs, 82 targets, `unmatched_symbols == []`. This is the
   pin that reddens if the CSV is edited carelessly.

**Route:** 200 without a bearer token; `test_ui_serving.py`'s ordering/auth assertions stay green;
`test_image_contents.py` stays green.

**User (vitest), `CancerAssociations.test.jsx`:**
1. a 3-association fixture renders **all three, in descending score order** — assert the order, not
   just presence (this is the contract that makes "all of them" readable);
2. a 16-association fixture renders **all sixteen** — no truncation, no "show more" swallowing rows;
3. a 1-association target reads correctly in the singular;
4. the claim-boundary text renders;
5. the derivation-gap note renders, and `337` / `82` / per-target counts appear **only** from the
   fixture payload — use distinctive fixture values (e.g. 9 pairs, 4 targets) and assert the live
   literals are absent;
6. a target missing from the map renders "no association recorded", not a blank.
