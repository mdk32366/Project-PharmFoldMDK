## §2 — The new item: the local fold envelope (D-077, staged)

**The owner's observation, 2026-08-04:** *the census can be sliced by one more dimension — ability
to fold on the laptop. All that costs is power.*

**What reading the code turned that into.** `core/manifest.py:51-54` records the local ceiling as
**known-good 440, known-bad 630, band UNMEASURED**, and **D-024 left it open deliberately, calling
it cheap.** Thirteen cohort targets route to paid rental compute on the reason string
`unmeasured_local_ceiling` — including **ENTPD1 at 441 aa, one residue over a bound nobody has
measured.** The instrument to close it already exists (`worker/ceiling_probe.py`, D-022, pure
bisection already unit-tested, crash-resilient, writes only JSONL and never touches the database).

So this is not a build. It is **running an existing tested instrument on a different card**, plus
four small pieces of new work. It is the cheapest open item on the project.

### Why it is worth a session slot, in order of value

1. **It creates the overlap F-008 said did not exist.** Tier was assigned by length; precision by
   tier; so length, tier, and precision are one partition with **no overlap**, and D-075 decision 6
   explicitly declines to resolve it. If the measured ceiling exceeds 440, targets folded on rental
   at **fp16** become foldable locally at **int8** — *the same target at two precisions.* That is
   **F-008's own named resolution path**, and Matt's observation makes it cost power. ⚠ It is **not
   run today** — it can move F-004, so it is separately pre-registered as **D-078** before any such
   fold happens.
2. **It gives Phase 2 a cost instrument that works before the money is spent.** Foldability is a
   *pre-fold, sequence-only* predicate. The roadmap's census (3.1) currently cannot answer *"what
   does a census of N targets cost?"* This answers it for arbitrary N without folding anything.
3. **It gives the paper a free reproducibility claim.** *"M of the folds underlying this result are
   reproducible on a consumer 8 GB GPU with no cloud spend."* That is a real strength of the
   single-sequence / no-MSA design, and nobody states it because most pipelines can't.
4. **It closes D-024's open item** and converts 13 routing decisions from guess to measurement.

### ⚠ The guard, which is the more important half

**Local-foldability is a monotone step function of ECD length — which is feature 1.** It is exactly
collinear with tier and with precision. Left unlabelled it re-imports F-008 under a new name and
smuggles a compute-budget variable into a biology ranking.

**D-077 decision 1 rules it, before the measurement, as three refusals:** it may not become a model
feature; it may not sit beside suitability without its label; **it may not filter the census** — an
affordability-filtered census is a census of our budget, biased by length, which is the F-009 error
one level out. What it legitimately is: a **cost / tractability / reproducibility** axis, and the
only one computable before folding.

### The two things reading the code found

- **`worker/ceiling_probe.py` defaults `--dtype` to `fp16`** (it was written for the A6000). A local
  run that forgets `--dtype int8` measures a ceiling for a recipe the local tier does not use — and
  that number would be written into the constant that routes int8 folds. Two paths to one quantity,
  free to drift. D-077 dec 3 binds the constant to its recipe.
- **The probe cannot express a flaky boundary.** `bounds_from_history` raises the floor on any
  single `ok`; `next_probe_length` **raises `ValueError` when `bad <= good`**. So *ok@560 then
  oom@500* — entirely plausible **378 MiB** from the wall — makes the probe **crash rather than
  report the flakiness**. Not a defect (it was correct for a card far from its wall); a design
  assumption that may not hold here. D-077 dec 4 adds a k=4 repeat rule (inherited from the 630-aa
  4-for-4 precedent, not invented) and makes **`unstable` a pre-registered, reportable outcome**.

## §5 — The through-line

The sealed baton points at one run whose meaning was fixed before the numbers existed; that has not
changed and should happen first. What changed overnight is that the owner noticed a dimension the
project had been paying for without measuring — and reading the code found that the project had
*already decided* to leave that measurement open and cheap (D-024), *already built* the instrument
for it (D-022), and *already recorded* that its two most load-bearing assumptions — a sharp boundary
and a fixed chunk size — were never tested. The cheapest observation on the project turned out to
sit on top of an unmeasured constant that routes real targets to paid compute, and on a possible
route to the one confound D-075 explicitly cannot resolve. **The value is in the measurement; the
discipline is in refusing, in advance, to let a compute-budget variable become a biology axis.**
