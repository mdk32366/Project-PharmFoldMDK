## §4 — Tranche 6 design document — the gating deliverable

Subjects: **FAT1–4, LRP1/1B/2, USH2A, ADGRV1, PKHD1L1** — stacks of independently-folding domains,
each inside the trained context. ⚠ **A single 4,400-residue pass would be the questionable choice
even on infinite VRAM.**

It must settle, **before anything folds**:

1. **The domain-boundary source** — UniProt `Domain` vs Pfam vs InterPro. ⚠ **They will not agree,
   and picking one after seeing results is how a boundary gets chosen for the answer it gives.**
2. **Per-domain span rules**, and what happens to inter-domain linkers — folded, omitted, or a named
   category.
3. **The assembly step** — and whether an assembled model is *a structure* or *a set of structures*.
4. ⚠⚠ **A `boundary_method` that says so.** Assembled artifacts are **not comparable** to
   single-pass folds and must never share a column with them silently — **F-037 one level up.**
5. **How pLDDT is reported for an assembly.** ⚠ A mean over concatenated domains is not a mean over
   a structure.

**Suggested first move, cheap and cache-only:** count the domains each of the 10 has under each
candidate source and check whether they agree. ⚠ **That answers whether the boundary source is even
a live question before designing around it** — and it needs no GPU and no network beyond cache.
