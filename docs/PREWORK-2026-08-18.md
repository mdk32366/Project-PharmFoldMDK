## §3 — ⚠ FIRST JOB: the tranche 6 design document

**Owner ruling D-091 put this in front of everything**, including the 566 cheap rental rows.

Subjects: **FAT1–4, LRP1/1B/2, USH2A, ADGRV1, PKHD1L1** — stacks of independently-folding domains,
each inside the model's 1,026-residue trained context. ⚠ **A single 4,400-residue pass would be the
questionable choice even on infinite VRAM.**

It must settle, **before anything folds**:

1. **The domain-boundary source** — UniProt `Domain` vs Pfam vs InterPro. ⚠⚠ **They will not agree,
   and picking one after seeing results is how a boundary gets chosen for the answer it gives.**
2. **Per-domain span rules**, and inter-domain linkers — folded, omitted, or a named category.
3. **The assembly step** — is an assembled model *a structure* or *a set of structures*?
4. ⚠⚠ **A `boundary_method` that says so.** Assembled artifacts are **not comparable** to
   single-pass folds and must never share a column with them silently — **F-037 one level up.**
5. **How pLDDT is reported for an assembly.** ⚠ A mean over concatenated domains is not a mean over
   a structure.
6. ⚠ **How it interacts with `F-040`.** Several of these are oligomers; an assembled monomer stack
   inherits *both* caveats.

**Suggested first move — cheap, cache-only, no GPU:** count the domains each of the 10 has under
each candidate source and check whether they agree. ⚠ **That answers whether the boundary source is
even a live question before designing around it.**
