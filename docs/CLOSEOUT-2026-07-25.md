## 1. The arc, honestly framed (D-042 → D-050)

The through-line is one sentence: **as the cohort nearly doubled, each layer that reported on it had
to be made to track the live data rather than a frozen moment.** Three things had silently detached
from the growing cohort, and this session reattached each:

- **The recipe had detached from the fold (D-042 → D-047).** D-042 corrected the rental recipe
  (`chunk_size` `None`→`64`) in the *table*, but the recipe was snapshotted into each job at enqueue
  and never refreshed — so the 2026-07-24 rerun requeued five pre-D-042 jobs and faithfully replayed
  the stale unchunked recipe, re-OOM'ing. **D-047** moved recipe resolution to **fold-time**
  (`build_fold_spec` reads the current `TIER_RECIPE[tier]`, not the frozen `inference_settings`), so
  no enqueued job can carry a stale recipe again. `TIER_RECIPE` relocated to `core/contracts.py` so
  the serving tier resolves it without importing `worker/` (DEP-001); `requeue_jobs` gained a
  fail-loud tier guard. Regression red→green on the exact bug.

- **The UI had detached from the fold's environment (D-048).** UI-depth §3 shipped on the D-046
  harness, tests-first: the two-population provenance panel (§3.1, captured vs. "not captured"),
  tier legibility (§3.2), the per-residue pLDDT spread (§3.4), and the band-contract pin (§3.3).
  The provenance red→green audit is on the record — the 9 tests ran red (4 failed) against the old
  em-dash panel, green after the rewrite; they bite.

- **The bands had detached from the distribution (D-049).** D-039's 50/60/70 were justified on the
  42-fold distribution. The disqualifying query showed the shape had moved: the divider held but its
  justifying number did not (45%→29.1% below 60), and the cohort max had moved 81.4→84.23. The
  D-048 band-contract pin **caught the stale constant** (red-then-green on 81.4→84.23) — a ruled
  constant changing the way ruled constants must: visibly, through a failing test.

- **The copy had detached from the count (D-050).** The pre-demo walk found the most dangerous gap
  this project can have: **three different "how many did you fold" numbers on the deployed app at
  once** — 67 on `/coverage` (computed), 40 on `/method` (hardcoded), 42 on the ADC panel
  (hardcoded). The fix was structural, not a re-typed number: `AdcContext` and `MethodNote` now
  **derive** their statistics from `/api/analyses` and `/api/coverage` the way `CoverageLine` and
  `TargetList` already did. The contradiction is eliminated, not re-stated — the copy moves with the
  data now.

The reason-from-the-artefact discipline carried the whole session: the recipe bug was read off the
OOM's `at chunk_size=None` string; the band shift off the live `/api/analyses` fetch; the
three-number contradiction off the deployed app, not the code.

## 5. The demo's strongest asset — the honesty is the pitch, hand it forward ready to use

Two questions a DL grader will ask are now the project's **best** moments, not its weak ones,
*because* the answers are deliberate rather than defensive. Lead with these:

- **"Why is your confidence divider at 60?"** — *Measured on the current 79-fold cohort: 29.1% fall
  below it (named query, dated). It's a convention anchor (50/70) plus a line the cohort's own mass
  justifies, and when the cohort grew we re-ran the query rather than keep a stale number.* (D-049.)
- **"Your best structure is 84 pLDDT — isn't that mediocre?"** — *Yes, and the UI says so: no target
  reaches the high-confidence range, and the top-band caveat states it. We report the ceiling
  honestly rather than hide it.* (D-039/D-049.)
- **"What's the lowest, and why show it?"** — *PTPRZ1 at 30.68, rendered "very low." Hiding failures
  is the violation; showing them with their band is the discipline.* (D-024.)
- **"Two targets aren't folded."** — *IGF2R (a documented A6000 ceiling, chunk-invariant OOM) and
  MUC16/FAT2 (named oversize, D-022) — named with reasons, not silent gaps.*

The frame to carry: **conviction about the mechanism, precision about the limits.** A system honest
about which is which is the more persuasive artefact, not the more timid one.
