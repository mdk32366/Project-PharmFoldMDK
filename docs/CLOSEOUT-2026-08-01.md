## 1. The correction — a superseded score and its narrative reached the grader

The single most important item, recorded in the project's explicit-correction discipline (never a
silent fix).

**How known (D-016):** the Razzak meeting summary reports NECTIN4 as *"scored 20.2655, ranked 8 of
56, top 20% percentile,"* framed as validation (effective, minimal side effects). Read against the
committed result `F-004-result-and-D-062-amended-orders.md` (`ranking_run` id=2,
`scorer_version=91e646e4a289`) and the curated `data/adc_reference_mapping.csv` (12 positives,
NECTIN4 = Q96NY8).

**Two parts:**

- **The number was a fusion of three real figures.** Committed truth: NECTIN4 at **LOO percentile
  0.848, ranked 4th of the 12** labelled positives. The spoken "8 of 56" collapsed the head-to-head
  denominator (8), the full ranking set (56), and a stale **20.2655** from a pre-F-004 exploratory
  scoring pass the committed fit had superseded. **No integrity failure** — the number came from a
  model that was *replaced*, not one that didn't exist. Memory-drift, recorded not buried.
- **The frame was the pre-F-004 one, which F-004 forbids.** F-004 caveat (c): NECTIN4 in the top
  four is *"consistent with signal and equally consistent with"* the pLDDT-attention confound, and is
  *"not narrated as validation."* The committed headline is a null that fired — the structural axis is
  orthogonal to the comparator but cannot be shown to add anything at n=12. **The standing risk is the
  frame, not the number:** any deck or talk leading with NECTIN4-ranks-high reintroduces the confound
  F-004 exists to hold. The artifacts below all lead with the null instead.

## 3a. The Grok second opinion — its best punch landed, and became D-075

The adversarial review ran (Team-of-Experts mode). **Its scores are noise** (2/10 for a project that
pre-registered its own null is not a serious assessment), **but two of its questions are signal, and
one is the sharpest external challenge the project has had.**

- **The circularity attack (the one that matters).** The positive label is "attempted as ADC" =
  research history; two of six features are pLDDT-derived; ESMFold confidence tracks training-data
  representation → research attention → attempt history. So the label and two features may share a
  common cause, and the 0.607 enrichment is *what "attention → pLDDT → rank" predicts on its own*.
  F-004 named this as an open caveat but **never ran the test that would settle it.** Grok named the
  test: ablate the pLDDT features and see if signal survives; or match negatives on
  publication/PDB-presence. **The honest pre-test status is not "orthogonal but unproven" — it is
  "orthogonal but unproven, AND the attention-artifact explanation is not excluded."** That second
  clause was missing from F-004 and is now the point.
- **Response: D-075, a pre-registered ablation** (staged). Refit the L2 logistic on the four
  non-pLDDT geometric features **plus one pLDDT-free accessibility proxy** (membrane-proximal SASA
  from raw coordinates, confidence-blind, with a red-then-green test proving it never reads the
  pLDDT column). Then a popularity-matched control on **both** PDB-presence and publication count.
  **Both outcomes pre-registered before it runs:** survival hardens the claim; collapse converts a
  hidden weakness into a stated finding and is *better found by us than by the room*. Either way the
  project is stronger — the only losing move is not running it. **This is the highest-leverage item
  on the project right now.**
- **Where Grok overreached, recorded so the score isn't over-weighted:** "uninterpretable" proves
  too much — the n=12 underpowering is *ours*, pre-registered, and F-004 already declines the
  significance claim Grok attacks. And the novelty search **confirmed** the empty intersection
  independently (even surfacing a paper we lacked — **PNAS 2026 surfaceome mapping**, now the true
  nearest neighbour, to be added to the lit review with a site-level-vs-target-level distinction).
  Its "manufactured gap / incremental" critique is partly fair and is absorbed: pitch this as *the
  first honest measurement of an under-explored axis*, **not** a methodological advance.

## 3b. F-009 — the cohort is the comparator, not a census (CD30, CEACAM5, Trop-2)

Owner raised: CD30 (approved ADC since 2011, brentuximab vedotin) and CEACAM5 (phase-3 tusamitamab
ravtansine) are real, clinically-validated ADC targets **absent from the 82.** Confirmed absent
(grep) and confirmed clinical (web search). **The finding, staged as F-009:**

- The 82 is **Kathad's expression cohort, used as the comparator** — not a complete target list. "Why
  isn't CD30 here" answers cleanly: it isn't in Kathad's cohort, and the fold set must match the
  comparator for the delta to mean anything.
- **The sharper point turns the gotcha into support:** CD30, CEACAM5, and Trop-2 (already on record)
  are three clinically-validated targets Kathad's expression-and-selectivity filters **excluded.**
  Expression filtering dropping approved/phase-3 targets is concrete evidence the expression axis is
  incomplete — which is *why* an orthogonal axis is worth measuring. Three false negatives motivate
  the project; they don't undermine it. **But this only works said first**, not when the room finds it.
- **Guard (connects to D-075):** do NOT claim "our method would have caught them." Unfolded, unscored,
  and CD30's 2011 approval makes it maximally attention-rich — using it to validate the scorer walks
  into the confound. Indict the comparator, not this project's scorer; keep them separate.
- **Future work this surfaces (a possible answer to Grok's sinking question):** a held-out label set
  of *known-positive ADC targets Kathad excluded* (Trop-2, CD30, CEACAM5, +sweep) would be closer to
  clinical validation than attempt-history AND a set the attention-confound doesn't automatically
  explain — exactly the "independent biological label" Grok demanded. Logged unbuilt.
